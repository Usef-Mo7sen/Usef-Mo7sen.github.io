Case 2: 4 Unit Zirconia Bridge

Buccal & Lingual View

  • Emergence Profile: Controlled emergence angles designed to manage tissue displacement over an extended edentulous span without impinging on the residual alveolar mucosa.
  • Inter-Pontic Embrasure Hygiene: Specifically sculpted middle embrasure between the two pontics, allowing through-and-through superfloss threading without compromising structural bulk.
  • Quadrant Continuity: Uniform alignment of the extended buccal corridor matching the adjacent dentition.

Occlusal & Intaglio Surface

  • Dual-Pontic Architecture: Dual Modified Ridge Lap contours featuring distinct, convex contact points separated by an open inter-pontic hygiene tunnel.
  • Marginal Integrity: Strict circular margin adaptation on terminal abutments to resist increased leverage forces across the extended span.
  • Structural Rounding: Smooth transitions along all line angles to prevent stress riser formation under elevated flexural load.

Buccal Occlusal Relation

  • Narrowed Occlusal Table: Buccolingual table width reduced by 10% to 15% on pontic units to minimize vertical vector forces and torque on the terminal abutments (Ante's Law compliance).
  • Static Centric Contacts: Pin-point, non-deflective centric stops without heavy primary occlusal loading on the dual pontic span.
  • Plane Harmonization: Strict alignment with the Curve of Spee and Curve of Wilson to prevent premature posterior contacts.

Lingual Intercuspation

  • Complete Excursive Disclusion: Zero lateral or protrusive working/non-working contacts on either pontic unit to protect the long span from destructive shear forces and bending moments.
  • Stress-Free Central Locking: Stable tripodized holding contacts solely dedicated to axial load absorption.

Engineering & Material Standards (High-Flexure Management)

  • Connector Hierarchy: Asymmetrical connector design:
    • Terminal abutment connectors: ≥ 9 to 10 mm².
    • Central inter-pontic connector: Reinforced to ≥ 12 mm² (3 mm × 4 mm vertical dimension) to counteract the 8× flexural bending risk (Δ ∝ L³).
  • Structural Wall Thickness: ≥ 1.0 mm axial walls on terminal abutments and ≥ 1.5 mm occlusal thickness on all units.
